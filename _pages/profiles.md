The High Energy Physics and Instrumentation Laboratory at Banaras Hindu University focuses on frontier research in experimental particle tracking architectures, micro-pattern gaseous detector setups, silicon sensors, and collider data analytics at CERN (CMS) and future facilities like the EIC.

### Group Openings & Recruitment

Our laboratory is entering a transition phase as our senior scholars conclude their doctoral tracks. We are actively seeking **highly motivated, independent Research Scholars** to spearhead our next generation of instrumentation and collider analytics frameworks.

##### 🎯 Who We Are Looking For:
* Candidates holding a strong rank in **CSIR-UGC NET (JRF) / GATE** in Physics.
* Scholars with an interest or background in core computational pipelines (**C++, Python, ROOT**, or finite-element simulation tools like **ANSYS/Garfield++**).
* Individuals eager to contribute directly to data analysis loops or hardware R&D tasks associated with international mega-science collaborations (CMS at CERN / Electron-Ion Collider).

##### ✉️ How to Apply:
If you are driven to explore experimental high-energy physics, please send an email directly to **ajay.phy@bhu.ac.in** with the subject line `Prospective Scholar - [Your Name]`. Please enclose a concise CV detailing your academic history, qualification scores, and a brief description of your coding or laboratory instrument exposure.
